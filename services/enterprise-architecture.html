Connected Enterprise Design

Trace strategy across every architecture layer

Enterprise architecture connects business intent to data, applications and technology, creating one coherent view of the changes and investments needed to achieve strategic outcomes.

1. Start with business direction

Strategies, value streams and capabilities define what the organisation needs to achieve. Business architecture establishes the requirements that every supporting layer must serve.

2. Connect data and applications

Information and application views show what data is required, where it is mastered and exchanged, and which systems enable or constrain priority business capabilities.

3. Shape the technology foundation

Technology, security and integration blueprints identify the platforms and standards needed for reliable delivery while revealing duplication, technical debt and transition dependencies.

How connected architecture improves decisions

  • Tests whether technology proposals directly support business outcomes.
  • Reveals shared dependencies and opportunities across separate initiatives.
  • Balances short-term delivery with security, scalability and adaptability.
  • Sequences change through practical transition architectures.

One source of direction

The result is a traceable target architecture and roadmap that helps business and technology leaders make consistent investment decisions using the same strategic context.
