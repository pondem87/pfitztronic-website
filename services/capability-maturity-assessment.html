Blueprint-Led Assessment

Measure maturity in the context of strategy

Business architecture blueprints ensure maturity is assessed where it matters—not as an isolated score, but against the capabilities and outcomes required by your strategy.

1. Identify strategic capabilities

Capability and value-stream blueprints trace strategic objectives to the parts of the business responsible for delivering them, defining a relevant assessment scope.

2. Assess the enabling dimensions

Each priority capability is evaluated across people, process, information, governance and technology using evidence, stakeholder input and consistent maturity criteria.

3. Prioritise the maturity gaps

Heat maps expose weak capabilities and cross-capability dependencies. Gaps are ranked by strategic impact, risk, urgency and the effort required to close them.

Result: an evidence-based improvement roadmap

Leaders gain a traceable line from strategy to capability maturity, enabling investment in the improvements most likely to strengthen performance, resilience and growth.
